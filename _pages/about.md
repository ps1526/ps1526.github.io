I am an undergrad Data Science student at UCSD with a Math minor. My research interests range from synthetic 
voice generation using autoencoders to geometric deep learning and neural algorithmic reasoning. 

Recently, my current research has been focused on how we can align neural architectures to specific 
properties such as physical inductive biases, classical algorithms, and geometric properties.

Currently I am working on a couple of cool things with cool people!

I am collaborating with:

- **Prof. Yusu Wang** on neural algorithmic reasoning and reinforcement learning for combinatorial optimization (how to align models with algorithms to solve hard problems)
- **Prof. Rex Ying** on non-Euclidean foundation models (how to leverage geometric properties for better representation)
- **Prof. Andrea Chiba** on building audio pipelines and models for stress detection in children (how to use voice as a non-invasive biomarker in the classroom)

I also have worked on building benchmarks for graph information retrieval as a Ledell Scholar at UCSD, as 
well as building knowledge graphs for pharmacology databases.

Other related interests are geometric deep learning, manifold learning, and any subfield of math!.

You can probably find me at the lab, at a cafe, or in my bed!
